# Ocean theme artwork

Both raster assets were created with the built-in image_gen tool and copied into the project. Sea animals, waves and bubbles use SVG/CSS animations.

## School banner

Saved asset: `public/images/ocean-school-hero.png`.

Prompt: Wide 3:1 landscape hero banner for a school portal ocean theme, polished luminous stylized digital illustration. Tropical turquoise ocean with underwater sunbeams and subtle water caustics. RIGHT THIRD: a beautiful white school building on a small lush palm-fringed island under blue sky and fluffy clouds, no writing on the school. LEFT TWO THIRDS: deep azure blue teal underwater negative space for white heading overlay, vivid cyan surface light above, gentle waves and underwater coral silhouettes along the lower edge. Strong rich ocean blue and turquoise palette, like premium aquatic adventure artwork, elegant high detail, beautiful clear water, not cartoon flat. No text no letters no logos no people no animals (animated animals will be added in code). Save local output path.

## Coral seabed

Saved asset: `public/images/ocean-coral-seabed.png`, with transparent background.

Prompt: Transparent-background wide underwater coral reef foreground strip for bottom of an ocean-themed website, 3:1 landscape. Gorgeous polished stylized aquatic illustration: sandy pale turquoise seafloor across bottom, orange branching coral and purple anemones clustered at LEFT CORNER and RIGHT CORNER, green seaweed, smooth dark blue rocks, small orange starfish. LOW foreground silhouette, taller coral near corners only, center mostly low sandy floor. Entire top half genuinely transparent alpha, no rectangular sea background, no water surface, no lettering, no fish or animals (animated separately), no humans. Glowing cyan rim light, vivid luxurious ocean-adventure illustration matching a tropical turquoise school portal hero. Full asset reaches bottom and side edges. Save local file path.

Decorations activate only for Ocean, never intercept controls, pause when the tab is hidden, stop with reduced-motion preferences, and are hidden in print. Animals enter from varied edges and corners, and are replenished after cartoon shark encounters. The interactive shark preserves its position across pages. See `docs/ocean-shark.md` for lifecycle details.
